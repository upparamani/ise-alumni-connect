Alumni Privileges & Campus Benefits

Exclusive recognition and campus access for contributing KLS GIT ISE alumni.

🎟️

AURA National Cultural Fest — VIP Alumni Passes

Active alumni delivering webinars or mentoring receive 2 Complimentary VIP Passes to KLS GIT's flagship national festival, AURA, with reserved seating at the Open Air Amphitheatre.

🏢

Campus Executive Guest House Stay & Dining

Alumni visiting Belagavi for on-campus guest lectures, hackathon judging, or board reviews are hosted with 1–2 Days Complimentary Stay & Dining at the GIT VIP Campus Guest House.

🎯

Direct Talent Pipeline & Campus Hiring Priority

Alumni founders and hiring managers get first-track access to pre-final and final-year ISE students for internships, campus referrals, and pre-placement interviews.

📜

Institutional Felicitation & Industry Advisor Citation

Formal certificate of appreciation from the Principal & HoD, inclusion in Department Advisory Board (DAB) / BoS consultations, and public recognition on official college media channels.

📚

Lifelong Incubation & Digital Library Access

Alumni entrepreneurs receive free access to the KLS GIT Incubation Center (EDC), co-working spaces during campus visits, and IEEE digital library access.
