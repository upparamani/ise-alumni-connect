KLS Alumni Legacy & Admission Assistance

Educational concessions & priority guidance across Karnataka Law Society institutions.

“Once a Student of KLS GIT, Always Part of the KLS Family.”

In recognition of alumni contributions to student webinars, mentoring, and department growth, the KLS Educational Trust extends admission assistance and institutional legacy fee concessions for children and immediate family members.

Eligible KLS Sister Concerns & Institutions

1. KLS Gogte Institute of Technology (GIT)

B.E. (All Branches), M.Tech, MBA, MCA, Ph.D. — Belagavi

2. KLS IMER (Management Education)

Full-time MBA & Management Programs — Belagavi

3. KLS Raja Lakhamgouda Law College

3-Year LL.B & 5-Year B.A. LL.B — Belagavi

4. KLS Gogte College of Commerce (GCC)

B.Com, BBA, BCA, M.Com & Pre-University — Belagavi

5. KLS Schools & Pre-University

KLS Public School, B.K. Model School, PU Colleges

6. KLS VDIT, Haliyal

Engineering & Technology Campus — Haliyal

Tiered Privilege Structure

Tier 1: Direct Kin (Children of Alumni)

10% to 15% Tuition Concession under Institutional/Management Quota as an "Alumni Ward Legacy Scholarship", plus priority hostel allotment on campus.

Tier 2: Immediate Siblings (Brothers / Sisters)

5% to 10% Concession on tuition or flexible semester fee installment options across KLS degree and PU programs.

Tier 3: Alumni Referrals & Counseling Desk

Direct 1-on-1 admission counseling with senior faculty, fast-track document processing, and zero application fee for institutional quota.
